Accordion
Wind and solar capacity scale from today's base by the multiplier set on the Supply Capacity card; interconnector flows are capped at 1.1x today's rating.
Demand growth scales the whole half-hourly load curve; peak heat-pump load is added as a flat addition on the coldest winter evening only.
Gas covers demand net of nuclear, biomass, hydro and interconnector imports -- battery discharge is subtracted before the gas requirement is calculated.
